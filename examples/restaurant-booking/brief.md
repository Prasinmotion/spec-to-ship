# Table bookings: what we're building for you

Guests book your busiest tables and don't turn up. You lose roughly 3–5 tables a night that walk-ins could have filled, but nobody counts it yet.

## What it will do for you
- Count no-shows, so we know the real loss.
- Send WhatsApp reminders and make cancelling or rebooking easy, with no deposits or fines.
- Within 3 months of reminders: 1–2 lost tables per busy service instead of 3–5, 20% more bookings, bills 5% bigger.

## When
- No-show counting: 3–4 weeks after we start.
- Reminders and rebooking: within 2–3 months.

## What it won't do (yet)
- Take deposits or charge for no-shows.
- Let walk-ins join a waiting list from their phone.
- Handle food orders, delivery or takeaway.

## Decisions we need from you
1. When can we start? Every date depends on it, and delay costs tables.
2. Can you share today's bookings and average bill from Petpooja? We need them before counting starts.
3. Does a guest giving their number on a phone booking count as agreeing to WhatsApp reminders? If not, phone bookings get none. Needed before reminders begin.
4. What's your exact budget within the agreed range? We need it to fix the scope and quote.
